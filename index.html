Engineering Change Process: Before and After

When a product's design changes, the change has to travel through engineering, purchasing, manufacturing and quality before the new version can be built. I mapped how that work actually flows at two manufacturing sites, measured where the time goes, and designed a faster future state.

Portfolio version: company names, people, systems and part details are generic. Times are realistic working-day estimates for illustration.

Tools and methods used

Process mappingWalked the process with each team and drew it as it actually runs, including the steps that are not written down.
Value stream mappingMeasured hands-on time and waiting time per step to get lead time and the share of time that adds value.
Waste identificationTagged each delay by lean waste type: waiting, rework, overprocessing, motion and handoffs.
KaizenTargeted fixes (red bursts) aimed at the biggest waits, not just the longest tasks.
Standard workStandard request form, results template and release checklist so every change runs the same way.
AI assistanceAI summarizes review comments and drafts work instructions from the redlined drawing; people review and approve.
